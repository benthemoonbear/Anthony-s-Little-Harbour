About the author

Hello, I'm Anthony

I grew up in a small West Cornwall town, and spent my whole childhood on its beaches and coastline. One of my fondest memories is Marazion beach. Not the postcard view in front of St Michael's Mount, but the more rugged stretch known as “Top Town” beach and its tiny harbour, “Top Tieb Harbour”. We spent many happy hours there swimming, walking and fishing.

My family has roots at St Michael's Mount, and I'm sure that coast is where these stories come from. Now that I'm all but retired, I finally have time to write them down. My aunt once told me to “grow up”, and I'm in no hurry to.

I hope the Little Harbour books bring a little of the atmosphere of the coast to children everywhere, especially those who haven't grown up beside the sea. The stories are brought to life by my illustrator, Tom Harding, and we swap ideas for every book.
